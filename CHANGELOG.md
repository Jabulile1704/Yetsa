## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-23

The first version worth calling a version. Everything below was built before
the project had a changelog, so this entry is written from the commit history.

### The idea

Yetsa plans around a **scaffold** — the fixed points of a week — rather than a
list of dates. Tasks are poured into the gaps, and the app reports what is
actually free. Everything stays on the device: no account, no server, no
network.

### Added

- **Today**, a monochrome grouped list: windows as sections, scaffold anchors
  as footers, and a strip showing free time per window so the evening is
  visible without scrolling. Finished windows collapse to one line each.
- **Morning run** — the get-out-the-door sequence measured against the door
  rather than the clock, reporting slack rather than a bare time.
- **Recurrence** that handles the awkward cases: fortnightly anchored to a date
  so a skipped week does not shift the weekday, weekly quotas as a count owed
  rather than a date missed, and intervals counted from the last tick.
- **Alarms** through AlarmKit, which sound past silent mode and a Focus. Wake,
  leave home and knock off, grouped so days sharing a time share an alarm.
- **A capped snooze** — switching on the wake-up also schedules an unsnoozable
  alarm at the out-of-bed time, so the chain cannot outrun the buffer.
- **Arrival detection** — a geofence round the workplace, searchable by name,
  that records the real arrival and compares it to the assumption.
- **A morning quote**, chosen from the date so the set runs through before
  repeating. Bundled with the app; nothing is fetched.
- **Export and erase** — JSON for everything, CSV for the tick history, and a
  deliberate way to destroy the lot.
- **An app icon**: a Y whose long arm is also a tick, generated by a script so
  the shape lives in one place.

### Engineering

- 37 unit tests covering the recurrence engine, the day planner, the snooze
  cap, the clock and the export.
- CI on every push and pull request.
- Architecture, ERD and use-case documents under `Docs`.

### Known gaps

Tracked on the board: no widget, no assistant, no leftovers tracker.
